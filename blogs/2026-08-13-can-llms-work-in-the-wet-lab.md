Benchling just published BenchBench-Protocol, a benchmark testing whether LLMs can troubleshoot wet lab protocols like expert scientists do. Even the best models (Opus 5 at 59.2%) miss the practical judgment that comes from hands-on lab experience, showing there's still a real gap between knowing biology and doing it.
